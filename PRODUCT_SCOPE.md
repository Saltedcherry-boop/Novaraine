# NovaRaine Product Scope

## Mission

Build a trading operating system that connects execution, psychology, finance and purpose.

## Compartments

### Focus

Execute.

### Trade

Record.

### Review

Reflect.

### Story Lab

Turn observations into evidence.

### Challenges

Progress through Stage 1A to Stage 4.

### Finance

Track every movement of money.

### Purpose

Allocate profit into meaningful buckets.

### Library

Knowledge system.

## Event Engine

One completed trade automatically updates:

- Trade
- Finance
- Ledger
- Challenge
- Story Lab
- Tax
- Purpose
